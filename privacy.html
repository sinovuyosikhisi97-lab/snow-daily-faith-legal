Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Snow Daily Faith Uploader is a project under development for uploading original motivational and faith-based short videos to accounts authorized by their owners.

Information we process

When you authorize a platform connection, the application may process your account identifier, authorization tokens, video files, captions, and upload status information.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare content, submit requested uploads, and troubleshoot failures.

Storage and sharing

Authorized credentials may be stored in secured automation settings. Content and technical data may be processed by GitHub and the relevant social media platform. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can revoke access through your social media account settings. You can request removal of project-held data through the project's GitHub repository.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, visit our GitHub contact page. Do not include private credentials in public issues.
