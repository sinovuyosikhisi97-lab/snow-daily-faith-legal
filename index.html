About Our Application

Snow Daily Faith Uploader is a content creation and publishing application developed for Snow Daily Faith ZA.

Our application helps create and publish inspirational videos, Bible reflections, motivational messages, and educational Wisdom of Words content.

How We Use YouTube

Snow Daily Faith Uploader uses Google OAuth to securely authorize video uploads to the channel owner's YouTube account.

The application requests YouTube permissions only for authorized video publishing. We do not request or store Google passwords.

Privacy and Transparency

We respect user privacy and use Google account authorization only for the functionality described above.
